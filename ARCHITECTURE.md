## Replacement swing candidate, 24 October 2026
The default swing entrypoint is src/daemon/replacementSwingDaemon.ts. The pure strategy, calendar, risk, evidence, capture, archive, replay and runtime modules live in src/lib/strategy/v6/. The active AI epoch and all financial history are scoped under brain:v6:run:<runId>:. Redis fences the owning portfolio lease, exact epoch and source snapshots when committing financial state and its durable ledger outbox. Funding transport runs separately from five-second protection. A read-only /api/brain projection distinguishes current evidence from historical AI/XSEC results. The AI-only archive/switch protocol is src/lib/admin/resetAiRun.ts. This candidate is not deployed; release gates are documented in docs/V6_IMPLEMENTATION_AND_RELEASE_PREVIEW_2026-10-24.md.

## 3 November 2026: pruned and self-learning
The legacy pre-v6 swing daemon, its crowding filter and its tests were removed, along with stale planning docs. The v6 daemon (`src/daemon/replacementSwingDaemon.ts`) is the only swing entrypoint. Under the default open paper gate (`V6_PAPER_GATE`), `src/lib/strategy/v6/learning.ts` rebuilds a learning book from every closed AI trade on each scan, with no LLM. It computes shrunk, time-decayed mean R per asset and direction; pre-v6 history counts at half weight. Each entry is sized at 0.5x–1.5x of base risk, and clearly losing pairs are paused. The book is exposed at `/api/brain` under `learning`. Decision reference keys now expire after 3 days instead of accumulating once a minute.
